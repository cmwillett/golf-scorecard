How to Use

Joining a Round

  1. Tap Join Round.
  2. For an Open Round, choose the round you're playing.
  3. For a PIN-Protected Round, use the join information provided by the host.
  4. Choose your team or player.
  5. If required, enter your scoring PIN.

During the Round

  • Tap Score to enter scores as you play.
  • Tap Leaderboard anytime to see the live standings.
  • Scores and leaderboard information update throughout the round.

Hosting a Round

  • Tap Host Round to create a new round.
  • Choose the game/scoring format.
  • Choose Open Round for casual games or PIN Protected for larger outings.
  • Share the round information with everyone playing.

Example Workflow

  1. Tap Host Round and enter the host PIN.
  2. Create the round with the course, game style, holes, teams, and players.
  3. After creation, write down or copy the Join Code.
  4. Send the Join Code to everyone playing.
  5. Send each team only their own team PIN.
  6. Each team taps Join Round, enters the Join Code, chooses their team, and enters their team PIN.
  7. Teams enter their own scores during the round.
  8. Anyone can tap View Leaderboard and enter the Join Code to follow the live standings.
  9. Use this Admin page to view PINs again, reset a team PIN, lock scoring, reopen scoring, or delete a test round.
